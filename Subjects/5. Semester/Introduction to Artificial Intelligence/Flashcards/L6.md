# L6 - Reinforcement Learning - Markov Decision Processes

## Flashcards

What is a Markov Decision Process (MDP)?::A reinforcement learning task with the Markov property: future state distribution depends only on the present state.

What is the Markov property?::The conditional probability distribution of future states depends only on the present state, not on the history.

What defines a finite MDP?::Finite state set $\mathcal{S}$, action set $\mathcal{A}$, and one-step dynamics $p(s',r\mid s,a)$.

What are the state-transition probabilities?::$p(s'\mid s,a)=\sum_r p(s',r\mid s,a)$.

What is the expected reward for $(s,a)$?::$r(s,a)=\sum_{s',r} r\,p(s',r\mid s,a)$.

What is an episodic task?::A task that ends at a finite time $T$ upon reaching a terminal state.

What is a continuing task?::A task that goes on forever (no terminal state).

What is the discounted return $G_t$?::$G_t = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\gamma^k R_{t+k+1}$, with discount rate $\gamma\in[0,1]$.

What is a policy $\pi(a\mid s)$?::A mapping from states to probabilities of selecting actions.

What is the state-value function $v_\pi(s)$?::Expected return starting in $s$ and following $\pi$: $v_\pi(s)=\mathbb{E}_\pi[G_t\mid S_t=s]$.

What is the action-value function $q_\pi(s,a)$?::Expected return of taking $a$ in $s$ then following $\pi$.

What is the optimal policy?::A policy $\pi_*$ is optimal iff it is greedy with respect to $v_*$ (or $q_*$).

What is the Bellman equation for $v_\pi$?::$v_\pi(s)=\sum_a \pi(a\mid s)\sum_{s',r}p(s',r\mid s,a)[r+\gamma v_\pi(s')]$.

What is the Bellman optimality equation for $v_*$?::$v_*(s)=\max_a \sum_{s',r}p(s',r\mid s,a)[r+\gamma v_*(s')]$.

What is policy iteration?::Alternating policy evaluation and policy improvement ("greedification") until convergence.

What is value iteration?::Combining evaluation and improvement in one sweep: $v_{k+1}(s)=\max_a \sum_{s',r}p(s',r\mid s,a)[r+\gamma v_k(s')]$.

What is the recycling robot example?::An MDP where the robot chooses search/wait/recharge based on battery level (high/low) to maximize cans collected.
